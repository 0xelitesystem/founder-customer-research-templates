# Contributing

These templates are opinionated. Contributions that sharpen the opinion are welcome. Contributions that water it down to "be more balanced" are not.

## What good additions look like

- A new template for a research situation not covered (e.g., reactivation interview, pricing change announcement test, beta user feedback)
- A new anti-pattern section to an existing template, with a real example of why the bad version produces useless data
- Tightening wording or removing repetition

## What doesn't fit

- Templates for research that produces vanity metrics (NPS surveys, customer satisfaction "out of 5" scales)
- "Best practices" lists with no concrete script
- Generic "customer-centric" language without specifics

## Format

Every template follows the same structure:

1. **Goal** - what you walk away with
2. **When to use / not use** - the conditions
3. **Setup** - the opening script
4. **Script** - the actual questions in order
5. **Anti-patterns** - table of bad alternatives with reasons
6. **What to do with the data** - the after-action work

Keep templates between 100 and 250 lines. If shorter, you're skipping necessary detail. If longer, the founder won't read it during prep.

## Style

- Direct second person ("you", "ask", "listen")
- No marketing voice
- No "framework" or "methodology" language unless you're naming a specific named technique (JTBD, Van Westendorp)
- Show one good question and one bad question side by side whenever possible
